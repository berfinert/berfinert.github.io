Privacy Policy

App: Y2K Virtual Pet
Last updated: November 7, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Y2K Virtual Pet does not ask for, collect, store on a server or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Your game progress

Your pet's name, level, outfits and other progress are saved only on your own device, inside the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet use

The app loads the game files and its fonts from the internet the first time it opens, then keeps a copy on your device so you can play offline. These requests contain no personal information.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.
